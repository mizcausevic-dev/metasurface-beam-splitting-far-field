What the clock is doing

Frames 0–149

Input and aperture. A dielectric pillar array sits on a substrate. A circularly polarized field arrives at the aperture. This beat establishes the assembly. It does not yet claim a far-field result. The push-in is a scale change.

Frames 150–299

Designed spatial phase. Each drawn pillar has a cross-section and an in-plane rotation. Propagation phase follows the cross-section. Geometric phase follows the rotation, and the sign flips with circular handedness. The traces are a mathematical diagram on these drawn pillars, not the paper’s layout and not a measured phase map.

Frames 300–509

Propagation pathway. The transmitted aperture field is what the far field sorts by direction. Cyan marks that transport. Gold marks the designed phase. Violet marks handedness, kept off the power path. Wavefronts are a schematic under time compression, not a computed field.

Frames 510–749

Lobes and leakage. Desired lobes are the pair this handedness was summarized as producing. Other transmitted orders, reflection, unconverted polarization, and absorption stay in the accounting. Their weights are not computed here, and they are not drawn as zero.

Frames 750–1019

Handedness and limits. Design 1, as summarized in a 2022 review of the numerical paper, places RCP at ±19° and LCP at ±42°. The player crossfades those two reported states. It does not sweep intermediate ellipticity. The closing frame holds both states, the open power accounts, and the uncertainty.

What this is

The reference is Khalid, Feng, Khan, Yuan, and Somekh, “All-dielectric metasurface designs for spin-tunable beam splitting via simultaneous manipulation of propagation and geometric phases,” Optics Express 30, 13459–13468 (2022). The abstract says they propose and numerically realize a splitter that sends a circularly polarized beam into two directions and moves those directions by switching handedness.

Shen and Huang’s 2022 review summarizes one design as silicon nanopillars on silica, a 400 by 400 nm unit at 780 nm, and simulated angles of ±19° for RCP and ±42° for LCP. Those angles are drawn as cited directions. This page does not recompute them, and it does not close an efficiency budget.

Read the full essay for the phase convention, the open power accounts, and the sentences this page will not say. The diagram and the mindmap are the same mechanism without the clock.
